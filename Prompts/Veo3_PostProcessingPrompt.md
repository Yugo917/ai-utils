
### 🎬 **Postprocessing Prompt (English Version)**

> **Goal:** Add life, emotion, and a natural influencer-style camera feel to the video.

---

#### 🧠 **Main Prompt:**

Add as much life and emotion as possible to the characters’ eyes and body language.
Their faces should feel truly expressive — realistic micro-expressions, natural eye blinks, subtle smiles, expressive eyebrows, and dynamic head and hand movements.
Ensure smooth and accurate **lip sync** during dialogue, perfectly matching lip movement with speech timing.
The **camera** should never be static — use a slightly handheld, vlog-style movement with subtle shakes, focus adjustments, and natural reframing, as if filmed by a YouTuber or influencer.
Lighting should remain soft and realistic, with a slight cinematic depth of field (gentle background blur).
The overall feel should be spontaneous, energetic, and authentic — like a real moment being captured rather than a staged scene.

---

#### 🎨 **Optional — Style and Visual Adjustments:**

* Visual style: **realistic, warm, and lively**
* Slight color saturation for a friendly and engaging tone
* Subtle digital grain, inspired by consumer vlog cameras (e.g., Sony ZV-E10, Canon G7X)
* Light autofocus variations to enhance the “amateur handheld” vibe
* Maintain smooth motion — avoid robotic or overly stabilized movements

---
